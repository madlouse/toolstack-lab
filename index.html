Who this is for

Clear fit beats broad hype. Here’s who we build for — and who we don’t.

For

  • Solo creators, freelancers, indie builders who want copyable AI workflows
  • Ops / PMs who need criteria to pick tools without the pitch deck
  • Viewers of practical AI tool demos who want the long-form companion page

Not for

FAQ

Straight answers. No pitch deck.

Do you take affiliate commissions?

Not in Week-1. When we do, every affiliate link will be disclosed on the page and in Disclosures.

Will you guarantee income or traffic?

No. We show workflows and tradeoffs. Results depend on your skills, audience, and tool fit. No income guarantees.

Are demos original?

Yes. Original commentary and original demos only — no stolen clips, no re-upload farms. Aligned with our YouTube track rules.

Is content AI-assisted?

Production may use AI assistance (drafting, VO, edit). We disclose AI-assisted production where platform or local rules require — see Disclosures.

Can I pay to get listed?

Not now. A future submit path may offer paid listing; it is not implemented and has no checkout in this kit. Join the waitlist for “notify when submit opens.”

Do you cover Polymarket / trading / wallets?

No. Out of scope permanently for this hub.

Is this financial advice?

No. Tool and workflow information only — not financial, legal, or investment advice.
