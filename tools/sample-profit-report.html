Read-only access — we cannot change anything in your store
只读权限 —— 我们无法修改您店铺中的任何内容

We connect only with read-only permissions: Shopify (read_* scopes), Square (*_READ scopes or your daily sales summary email), and Clover (Read orders / Read payments / Read inventory permissions). We cannot create, edit, refund or delete orders, products, prices, customers or payments.
我们只使用只读权限连接：Shopify（read_* 权限）、Square（*_READ 权限或每日销售汇总邮件）、Clover（读取订单/支付/库存权限）。我们无法创建、修改、退款或删除任何订单、商品、价格、客户或付款。

Reconciled daily against your back office
每日与您的后台核对

Every day, before the email goes out, we reconcile our totals against the figures in your platform's back office (Shopify admin, Square Dashboard, Clover Dashboard). If they differ by more than 1%, the report is held and not sent until the gap is explained.
每天发送邮件前，我们都会将计算结果与您平台后台（Shopify 后台、Square 控制台、Clover 控制台）的数字核对。差异超过 1% 时，报告暂停发送，直至查明原因。
