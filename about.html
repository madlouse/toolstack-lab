About · demos, not hype

Who we are

Toolstack Lab is an English-primary info hub for solo creators, freelancers, and indie builders. We review and compare real AI tools, publish copyable workflows, and stay honest about limits, cost, and when a human still wins.

Our promise

Demos and commentary you can trust — not hype decks.

We prefer screen-real workflows over feature laundry lists. Original demos and commentary only — no stolen clips, no re-upload farms. We call out cost, privacy, lock-in, and when to stop automating.

No income guarantees. No fake star scores, fabricated MRR, or “guaranteed ROI.” Affiliates, when used later, will always be labeled. See Disclosures.
